The primitives this design calls for. All but one are built today in src/ui/ and src/app/; anything that is not carries a marker, as the search field and the dropdown do. Each is drawn at rest, under the pointer, pressed, with keyboard focus, and disabled or selected where that applies — the states are drawn rather than described, so none can be forgotten. The rest specimen in each row is live: hover it and press it. Sizes are shown at 100%, where 1rem is 16px; everything scales from the token in the sheet before this one.

DropdownNew · replaces SegmentedOne choice among a few, named.
