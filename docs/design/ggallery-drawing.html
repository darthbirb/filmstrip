One mark, at every size, including 16. There is no reduced-frame variant and no boundary size. At 16px each frame is 2.1px and each sprocket hole 1.28px, so the seven frames stop reading as frames and read as a colour band inside the body — and that is the accepted behaviour of this mark at that size, not a defect to be fixed with a second composition. The body is opaque and stays opaque. The mark it replaced answered light grounds by having no ground of its own — nine squares with holes between them. This one is an object: film is dark, and a dark body on a light taskbar is what a strip of film looks like. So there is no light variant, no inversion, no plate and no border.
